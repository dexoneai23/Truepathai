YOUR DATA

Everything stays on your device.

TruePath runs entirely in your browser. There's no server collecting your habits, moods, or reflections in the background.

Nothing sent to a server

Your habits, mood logs, and reflections are stored locally in your browser, not on a remote database.

Nothing sold, ever

There's no ad network or data broker involved — TruePath isn't built on a business model that depends on your data.

Yours to clear anytime

Clearing your browser data removes everything TruePath has stored — there's no separate copy sitting elsewhere.

QUESTIONS

A few things people ask.

Is TruePath free to use?
Yes. There are no paid plans, subscriptions, or premium tiers — TruePath is free to use.
Do I need to create an account?
Signing in creates a session in your own browser so your dashboard is there when you come back. It isn't tied to a server-side account system.
Where is my data stored?
Locally, in your browser. Nothing is transmitted to a server or third party.
Can I lose my data?
Since everything lives in your browser, clearing your browser data or switching devices will reset TruePath. It's worth keeping that in mind.
Will TruePath ever show ads?
No. TruePath isn't built as a business, so there's no advertising or monetization built into it.
